Integrations in the no-hardware version: do Apple Health, Oura and cycle-app sync come in now, and on which side of the membership line? Why it matters, from the feature review. No screen reads the personas’ sync settings today: there is no Apple Health, Oura, cycle app or CSV export. Integration is men’s top purchase driver (40%); 47% of women rated cycle correlation a must-have; and “Open data first” is Alex’s lead. Marcus wants patterns from Apple Health sleep and workouts; Alex wants Oura, Apple Health and Garmin context as pattern inputs, and an API or scheduled export. Data privacy is on the brand document’s list of what has to be true to buy, which is the case for a free CSV export. They also rescue the paid side. Under the meal logging we expect, possible patterns are 0 for all three demo people. Patterns from sleep, workouts and cycle days need no meal logs, so membership has something to show occasional meal loggers. Our recommendation, from the review: basic connection and CSV export free; context patterns and an API in membership. A first step that needs no integration: a Period chip on Log symptoms and the check-in tags (Stress, Short nights), read against the first BM and form the same way dinner times are tested. Then Apple Health and Oura sleep and workouts, and cycle-app data, once sync is scoped. Now or later is Will’s call; none of it is built, and Profile shows the three apps as Not connected so the demo doesn’t promise it.

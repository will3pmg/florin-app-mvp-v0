The BM logging toggle: Quick (the tiles on the plus card, plus the form) or Full (the form only). Quick: plus, then a tile, saves the BM. That is two taps, the same as Bellyx, and it is the low-effort daily log the retention target depends on. The plus tile tied for the highest score in the review (4.4 of 5). Four of five review lenses found the same flaw: a tile always stamps the time of the tap. The computed example: Sarah’s first BM today is at 7:42 AM. If she taps the tile at 9:30 instead, the BM lands outside her usual 7:15 to 8:45 AM window, the Timing part for today drops from 98 to 64, and today’s FloraScore moves from 83 to 78. Timing is 30% of FloraScore. Source: the model at commit 475f259, Sarah, C, today’s BM moved to 9:30 and L.score recomputed. The review’s fix is a When row in the saved toast: Just now, 30 min ago, 1 hr ago, Earlier. A late tap keeps the right time without opening the form. Full only: every BM opens Log a BM, which always asks the time (it defaults to now and is editable) and shows the extras under the form: the blood, mucus and black flag, the comfort details and a note. It is slower, but nothing gets the wrong time. Recommendation: Quick, with the When row. Catching up an earlier day and a person’s first few BMs still open the form, as built. The review also asks to move the blood, mucus and black flag directly under the form tiles with a Teal edge so it is never below the fold. Which extras stay inline is a separate call for Will (our recommendation: keep the flag and Sudden urge to go, put Burning and Didn’t feel finished behind one tap).

Should one tap on a tile save a BM?

This decides what happens when a person taps plus to log a BM.

Quick tiles and form

We recommend

BM saved

What it is: Tap plus, then a tile, and the BM is saved, with a message that offers Add details and Undo.

Why do it: A usual BM takes two taps, so logging every day stays easy.

Why not: A BM logged an hour late gets the wrong time, and details like urgency get skipped.

Full form only

Save

What it is: The plus menu has one Log a BM row, and every BM goes through the form: when, consistency, details and a note.

Why do it: Every BM shows its time before saving, with room for details.

Why not: It takes more taps every time, and people who find logging slow log less.

We recommend Quick tiles and form, and adding time buttons to the “BM saved” message: Just now, 30 min ago, 1 hr ago, Earlier. It keeps the two-tap log and fixes the wrong time without opening the form.

Still open: Should every BM open the form for the first two weeks, while Florin learns the person’s usual? In the demo, only the first 5 do.

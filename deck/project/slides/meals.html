6

Meals are occasional. Will’s call on Oct 14: meal logging happens when people are having issues, so make it as simple as saying what you ate that day, with a big voice button or one text box. That is built: one box for the day, a 96 px mic that animates while listening, and the foods counted as chips. The demo plays a sample transcript, since an artifact can’t use the microphone. The meal sheet toggle reads Quick or One meal. Quick is that one box, said or typed. One meal logs one meal at a time: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Other, then the text and a time, which the late-dinner pattern needs. The data, from the model at commit 475f259, over the 90-day pattern window in C: With meals logged every day, Insights finds 3 possible patterns for Sarah, 4 for Marcus and 3 for Alex (Sarah’s include pizza, then a looser first BM, 20 of 22 times against 26 of 52 otherwise). With meals logged on rough days only, all three drop to 0. Meals are then on 36 of Sarah’s 85 logged days, 30 of Marcus’s 87 and 38 of Alex’s 84, all of them days with a symptom. Under 60% of logged days with a meal, the model holds every meal pattern back, because rough-day-only logs name ordinary foods by selection. In testing that named toast for Sarah purely because of which days got logged. FloraScore doesn’t move: 83, 83 and 84 in every meal mode, and Today no longer asks for a meal. So the paid feature people would most want is empty under the logging we expect. The recommendation, from the review: build the 14-day food check as a real trial with a fixed length, free to start and run, one line a day, a day counter and end date on Today, offered in the None state too. Its results become the membership reveal. Also from the review: when the transcript mentions dinner, show one optional “Dinner after 8 PM?” chip under the box, so the late-dinner pattern survives the one-box log.

How does a person log what they ate?

This decides the meal row in the plus menu (What you ate, or Meal when One meal at a time is picked) and the sheet it opens.

Quick, voice or text

We recommend

Today

What it is: One big microphone button and one box for the whole day: say or type what you ate, and Florin picks out the foods.

Why do it: It’s the least effort, so logging meals on ordinary days, not only on days with symptoms, stays easy.

Why not: The day has no meal times, so Florin can’t look at what tends to follow a late dinner.

One meal at a time

Lunch

What it is: Pick Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Other, type what you had, and set the time.

Why do it: Each meal keeps its time, so Florin can look at what tends to follow a late dinner.

Why not: It’s more steps for every meal, so people log fewer of them.

We recommend Quick, with a one-tap “Dinner after 8 PM?” button when the person mentions dinner, and a free 14-day food check (log every meal for 14 days, ordinary days included). People tend to write meals down only on days with symptoms, which can make everyday foods look linked to them, so logging has to be easy enough for every day.

Not the same as Demo person’s meals under Demo, which only changes how often the demo person wrote meals down.

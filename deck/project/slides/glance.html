Florin app refresh · Decisions · Oct 27

3

Every decision in one table, in the order the deck takes them: logging, screens, data styles, then the business calls. Each row has its own slide. Read across the Status column. “Built” means it is in the prototype already. “New” means the option was added for this round as a toggle. “Your call” or “your approval” means it is waiting on Will. Where the recommendations come from: Consistency C: every review lens that chose an option chose C (feature review, Log a BM). Quick BM logging with a When row: the review’s fix for the one-tap tile stamping the time of the tap. Meals: Will’s Oct 14 call (one box and a big voice button) plus the review’s recommendation of a free 14-day food check. Check-in on and free: Sarah scored it 5, and the strategist calls it the app’s one scheduled return. Home Score first: our lean from the review (Oura’s order rated 4 to 5 by Marcus, Alex and the strategist), with the review’s highlight fix, now built: Today’s sentence leads with what stood out. Day first is the option for Sarah’s case. History Calendar: the design critic’s ask, in place of a scroll of 375 entries for Sarah. Onboarding Short: 60% of men want setup under 10 minutes (review). Easy as a setting: stage two spec. Data style: our read, for Will to confirm. Ask Florin, membership, FloraScore and integrations: the review’s calls for Will. Ask Florin is members only in the demo today; one free answer a week on Free is our proposal, not built. Messaging: an open question in the review; we bring directions, not a recommendation.

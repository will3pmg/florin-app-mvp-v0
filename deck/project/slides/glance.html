The nine decisions, one row each, in the order they sit in the demo’s left bar. Each has its own slide next. 1. Consistency: how does a person describe a BM’s consistency? A, numbers 1 to 7; B, five words; or C, seven words. We recommend C, once counsel has read it. 2. BM logging: should one tap on a tile save a BM? Quick tiles and form, or the full form only. We recommend quick tiles and form, plus buttons to fix the time. 3. Home: what does Today show first? Score first or Day first. We recommend Score first. 4. History: how should History show past days? A list or a calendar. We recommend the calendar. 5. Weekly check-in: should the app ask how the week felt? On or off. We recommend on: four questions a week, free for everyone. 6. Meal sheet: how does a person log what they ate? Quick, voice or text, or one meal at a time. We recommend quick, and a 14-day food check. 7. Data style: how should Florin draw the numbers? Six styles: Florin charts, Rings, Field journal, Bento, Oura and Easy read. We recommend the Oura style on Today and the FloraScore page, and Field journal on detail pages. 8. Version: should the app offer larger text and simpler screens? Standard or Easy. We recommend offering Easy as a setting, with Standard as the default. 9. Onboarding: how much do we ask a new person? Short, or the full survey. We recommend Short, and asking the rest later, when the answer changes something. Also to settle: Ask Florin on Free. We recommend one free answer a week, with questions about seeing a doctor always answered. Two words used throughout. Usual: each person’s own pattern, learned from their own logs. Florin starts reading against it on day 14, and never compares a person with anyone else. FloraScore: the day’s score, made from four things a person logs: when the first BM comes, how often, consistency, and comfort (the symptoms they log). It never uses meals.

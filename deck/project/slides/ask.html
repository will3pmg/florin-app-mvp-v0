Florin app refresh · Decisions · Oct 27

13

Ask Florin is now interactive for members. It answers from the person’s own numbers, through Claude, and checks every answer before showing it. The engine, in order (stage two spec, M2): 1. Alarm words in the question (blood, black or tar-like stools, fever, vomiting that won’t stop, weight loss, waking at night to go) get the Teal “When to see a clinician” answer, primary care first, with the urgent-care line. Never interpreted, and never sent to Claude. 2. Requests to diagnose, prescribe, dose or predict get a fixed, kind redirect to what the logs show and to a clinician. 3. Otherwise Claude answers, given Florin’s rules and a facts packet computed from the model: usual ranges, 7 and 30 day counts, today’s FloraScore and what moved it, patterns found or held back, the latest check-in against the first weeks, the top symptoms and today’s logs. Two to four short sentences, numbers only from the facts, ending on one thing to log or try. 4. Every answer runs through Florin’s claims check and a numbers check: every number in the answer must appear in the facts. If either fails, the computed answer shows instead, marked “Checked against Florin’s rules”. Each answer has a “From your logs” disclosure listing the numbers it used, and the fine print. In the demo this runs on the viewer’s own Claude usage; where Claude isn’t available, the computed answers run on their own. The answer on the slide is an example composed from Sarah’s numbers in the model, not a captured reply: her week averages 83 against last week’s 80; by part, Timing added about 3 points (95 against 88), Comfort about 1 (93 against 88), and Form took about 1 off (94 against 98). The check-in is due tonight. The open call for Will: one answered question a week on Free, started from a card’s numbers, and never lock the clinician question. Marcus scored the hand-off 2 of 5, because a buyer who has been burned before never sees an answer before being asked to pay. The strategist flags Ask as the biggest claims risk once answers ship, which is why step 4 exists.

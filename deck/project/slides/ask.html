Do people on Free get any Ask Florin answers?

This decides what the Ask tab does on the free plan; members ask questions and get answers from their own logs.

How Ask Florin answers a member, in three steps

1

Signs to see a doctor come first. Ask about blood or a black stool, and Florin says to see a primary care doctor. It reads nothing into it.

2

No diagnosis, doses or predictions. Ask for one, and Florin gives a fixed reply that points to the person’s own logs and to a clinician.

3

The rest, from their own logs. Claude writes the answer and Florin checks it. If the check fails, Florin shows its own answer from the logs.

Members only

What it is: On Free, the Ask tab explains what Ask Florin does and the questions people ask, and offers membership.

Why do it: It’s the simplest: no free answers to pay for or check.

Why not: People never see an answer from their own numbers before they’re asked to pay.

One free answer a week

We recommend

What it is: On Free, a person gets one answered question a week from their own numbers, and questions about whether to see a doctor are answered on any plan.

Why do it: People see what Ask Florin does with their logs before paying, which makes the case for membership.

Why not: Each free answer costs us money to run, and some people will find one a week enough and never pay.

We recommend one free answer a week, with questions about seeing a doctor always answered. One answer from a person’s own logs sells membership better than a locked tab.

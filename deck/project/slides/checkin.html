Florin app refresh · Decisions · Oct 27

7

The weekly check-in toggle: On or Off. With it off, the whole app runs without it, so Will can see exactly what goes. What it is: four questions in our own words, about 60 seconds, answered with chips: belly pain this week, days it got in the way, bloating, and how settled bowel habits felt. Each answer is compared with the person’s first three answered check-ins. There is no score and no band name, which also keeps it clear of any licensed questionnaire. The reviews disagree most here. Sarah scored it 5: for her it is the screen that believes her. After a rough week it said “More than at first” in Gold, with no score or severity label. Alex scored it 2: four answer steps carry little signal against a baseline from his first three weeks that never moves. Marcus scored it 3 and misses a question on urgency and changed plans. The strategist calls it the app’s one scheduled return and wants it kept fully free. What the app loses with it off: the card on Today when it’s due, the check-in item in the plus menu, the check-in card on Insights, and the check-in day and time in Profile’s reminders. What it saves: four questions a week. Recommendation: keep it on and free on every plan. The review’s design fix is to open the saved view with a compact four-row table (question, first three weeks, this week, the change in words) and fold the 13-week strips behind each row. Sources: the feature review (scores and quotes); the stage two spec for what the toggle removes; the model’s check-in rule (L.baseline: the first three answered weeks).

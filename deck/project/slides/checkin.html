What it is: The check-in card on Today when it’s due, an item in the plus menu every week, and its trend on Insights.

Why do it: It’s the one place Florin asks how the week felt, not just what happened, and it gives people a reason to come back each week.

Why not: It’s one more task a week, and in a steady week the answers add little that the logs didn’t show.

Off

No check-in

What it is: No check-in anywhere: no Today card, no plus item, no Insights card and no reminder.

Why do it: The app asks less of people, and the week’s logs already show what happened.

Why not: Nothing asks how the week felt, so a hard week shows only as logs.

We recommend On, free on both plans. Four one-tap questions a week, in our own words, not a licensed questionnaire. It takes a minute and is the app’s one weekly reason to come back.

The question: should the app ask how the week felt? This decides whether the app has a weekly check-in: a few questions about the past week. What the check-in is: four questions about the last 7 days, each answered with one tap, in about a minute. 1. How much belly pain did you have this week? None, A little, Some, A lot. 2. On how many days did your gut get in the way of your plans? None, 1 or 2 days, 3 or 4 days, 5 or more days. 3. How much bloating did you have this week? None, A little, Some, A lot. 4. How settled did your bowel habits feel this week? Settled, Mostly settled, Up and down, Unsettled. Then an optional line: tags such as Travel, Stress or Short nights, and a note. Florin compares each answer with the person’s first three weeks and says About the same, More than at first, or Less than at first. No score. It’s due once a week, on a day the person picks (Sunday evening to start). It shows as a card on Today and an item in the plus menu, with one reminder. A person can skip a week, and it never blocks logging or Insights. On. What it is: the check-in card on Today when it’s due, an item in the plus menu every week, and its trend on Insights.

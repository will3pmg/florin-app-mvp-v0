Florin app refresh · Decisions · Oct 27

21

The membership line, from the feature review’s membership section. The demo has a Plan switch, Free or Member, so Will can see both sides. The verdict: the line is the right shape. Logging, Today, the FloraScore number, the check-in and 7 days of Insights stay free, and membership sells the explaining. As built, though, the best-selling part of the explaining, possible patterns, is empty for people who log meals only on rough days or not at all (0 for all three demo people, from the meals slide). And two things behind the wall belong on the free side. What to move, with the reasons: The basic 30-day clinician report (BMs, flags, symptoms, check-in answers) goes free. All five reviews asked for it. It is Sarah’s value proposition, and today the alarm note on Free offers a report that History locks, which puts a paywall in the middle of an alarm. A fuller 90-day report stays in membership. Starting and running the 14-day food check goes free, with its results as the member reveal. Without it, Free users can’t create the data membership then explains. One Ask Florin answer a week goes free, our proposal on the Ask Florin slide. It is not built: today Ask Florin is members only, and on Free the Ask tab is the membership sheet, with no answers. Membership then sells Ask Florin for every question. CSV export goes free. “Open data first” is Alex’s lead, and data privacy is on the brand document’s list of what has to be true to buy. Context patterns from Apple Health, Oura and cycle apps, and Food Discovery Trials, join membership. Integration is men’s top purchase driver (40%), and 47% of women rated cycle correlation a must-have. Lock controls on Free Insights drop from 18 to two (the range tabs and the patterns teaser). The paywall sheet should lead with what this person’s logs already hold, and draw their real score in the ring, not the fixed 72 it shows now. Price: under $10 a month, the ceiling people named in the research. The exact number, when the trial starts (day 14, or when the food check ends), and whether membership carries into Florin One are Will’s calls. We have not set a price.

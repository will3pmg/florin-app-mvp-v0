The question: how should Florin draw the numbers? This decides how FloraScore, the week and the Insights cards look, with the same numbers from the same logs in every style. The week card you flagged is redrawn in one set of marks used on every screen; the next slide shows it. The real choice is Florin charts everywhere, or Oura on Today and Florin charts everywhere else. Easy read is the Easy version’s own, and Rings, Field journal and Bento are here to compare. The next six slides show each style live. Florin charts. What it is: the dark green FloraScore card with the usual range as a bar, the week as one circle a day, and plain bar and dot charts. Why do it: each card says the person’s usual in words before the chart, and every card works the same way. Why not: it’s the most text of the six, with small charts on every card. Oura. What it is: a dark screen with FloraScore as a large round gauge and a small ring for each part of the score. Why do it: Oura users already know the layout, with the score as one big gauge. Why not: it breaks the chart rules in four places, starting with part rings that carry no numbers, and it reads as a copy. Easy read. What it is: one big number and one plain sentence on each card, in large type. Why do it: it’s the easiest to read, for anyone with reading glasses. Why not: it leaves out the shape of the data, so it’s thin for someone who wants depth. Rings. What it is: FloraScore as a ring that fills up, with the usual range marked on it, and a small ring for each day. Why do it: rings read at a glance, and a week of rings is easy to count. Why not: a ring filling toward 100 reads as a grade, and a ring a day gets small over 30 days. Field journal. What it is: the numbers written out, with plain charts on labelled axes, each with a caption. Why do it: it’s the most exact, so it suits detail pages and a clinician report. Why not: it’s the most reading, and its small captions are hard for older eyes. Bento. What it is: tiles of different sizes, one number each. Why do it: easy to take in at a glance, like phone widgets. Why not: tiles show numbers but not how they connect, and they crowd when text is large. We recommend one look everywhere, Florin charts, with Easy read only in the Easy version (decision 8). A card and its detail page then draw the same day the same way, and Data style folds into Version. Try it: Decisions, Data style, then the Today and Insights tabs. Florin charts is the first option on the toggle. Picking Oura turns the demo dark, unless you’ve picked a theme or Easy is on. The demo shows one style at a time, and detail pages don’t change.

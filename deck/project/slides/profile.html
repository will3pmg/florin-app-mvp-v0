Profile isn’t a toggle; it’s a new screen in this round, opened from the profile button in the Today header. This slide is so Will can check what it holds. From the top: the person (an initial, first name, and “Logging since Jul 13” or “Day N of 14” while Florin is learning). Your usual: the timing window, how often, the usual forms in the current consistency option’s words, and the FloraScore range, each “set from your logs since” a date; in the learning state it says what comes on day 14. About you: the onboarding answers, each editable. Reminders: a BM reminder time, and the check-in day and time only when the check-in is on. Logging: the consistency words with How to pick, the meal sheet and the BM extras. Display: “Larger text and simpler screens”, which turns on the Easy version, and the app theme. Connected apps: Apple Health, Oura and a cycle app, each shown as Not connected, because integrations are a decision for this version. Membership: plan status; on Free it opens the paywall. Your data: make a clinician report, export your logs, share anonymised logs for research (off by default and revocable), and delete my logs with a confirmation. Then help, terms, privacy, about Florin (wellness, not a medical device) and sign out. Two rules from today’s shipping profile (reference/shipping-app/onboarding-and-profile.md). Today’s Delete Profile button is red, which breaks the app colour rules; delete stays, quieter, with a confirmation. Today’s profile has a Demo Settings card (“Requires assessment every visit to Insights”); that belongs to the prototype page, not the app. Source: stage two spec, M1 Profile.

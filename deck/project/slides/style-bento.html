Bento lays the numbers out as tiles of different sizes. The FloraScore hero is a glass panel over an atmosphere made from the brand tokens (a Forest, Teal and Plum glow), with standard tiles around it. The FloraScore card: the glass hero with a Ray ring at 83 of 100, its usual range of 74 to 82 drawn on a thin outer track, “A little above your usual range”, and “This week, up 3 on last week”. Under it, two small tiles: the week’s average, 83, on a range strip against last week’s 80, and the last 7 days of FloraScore over the usual band, with Oct 8 left as a gap. In the app the glass rises in, the ring sweeps and the tiles follow. The chart: this week as seven day tiles that fill from the bottom, one dot per BM. On 4 of 6 logged days her BMs were within her usual; on 2 (Wednesday and Friday) they shifted; Thursday has no logs and stays an outline. Today is ringed. Eight BMs on six days. Strength: it scans fast, and its tile grid is what the review asked for to open Insights: a two-by-two summary (timing, how often, form, symptoms) with the full cards one tap away. Cost: DESIGN.md says glass only over photography. Bento puts glass over a gradient, so the rule needs amending if Bento is chosen. Our read also: tiles tend to repeat the same number in two places. Sources: the model at commit 475f259 (Sarah, C: L.scoreMoved, L.scoreSeries over 7 days, L.span over 7 days), and look-bento.js in the data looks library.

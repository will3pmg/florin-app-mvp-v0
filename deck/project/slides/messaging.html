Florin app refresh · Decisions · Oct 27

24

The one statement slide. The brand’s line to Marcus and Sarah is that Florin doesn’t ask you to track anything. That line was written for Florin One, the hardware. This version of the app, the one that ships without hardware, is manual logging: plus and a tile for a BM (two taps), one box and a mic for meals when something feels off, and the weekly check-in’s four questions. This is an open question in the feature review’s calls for Will: how do we describe the app before Florin One ships? We don’t have a recommendation. We bring three directions to react to, none of them final copy: 1. Say the effort plainly. Two taps a BM, and Florin does the reading: the usual, the score, the patterns. Honest about the logging, and it puts the work on Florin’s side. 2. Lead with what the logs give back: your own usual, a FloraScore read against it, and a report to bring to a clinician. The logging becomes the price of something concrete. 3. Keep “doesn’t ask you to track anything” for Florin One, and present this version as the start of your record, which Florin One later continues. This needs a decision on whether membership and the usual range carry into Florin One (both are open questions on the membership and FloraScore slides). Whatever the direction, the claims rules hold: no promise of outcomes, nothing read against a norm, Ask Florin never sounds like a doctor. Sources: the feature review (calls for Will, the messaging question), the review’s note that the one-tap tile is two taps, and the brief.

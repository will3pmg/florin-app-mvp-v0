How should History show past days?

This decides the History tab, where people look back and fill in a day they missed.

List

+ Add

What it is: Every day, newest first, each opening with its day mark and one line on how it sat against the usual, then its logs; a day with nothing logged is a gap with Add to this day.

Why do it: Every log is right there to read, and filling in a missed day takes one tap.

Why not: After a few months it’s a long scroll, so finding a month’s gaps and days outside the usual takes many screens.

Calendar

We recommend

What it is: A month on one screen: a Plum circle for a day within the person’s usual, a Gold circle for a day outside it, and the date alone for a day with nothing logged.

Why do it: A whole month fits on one screen, so gaps and days outside the usual show at a glance.

Why not: Seeing a day’s logs takes a tap, and a month of circles says less than the list about what each day held.

We recommend Calendar. One screen a month replaces a long scroll, and a tapped day reads just as it does in the list.

Still open: Keep a switch on History so a person can pick the list or the calendar?

Try it in the demo: Decisions › History

Oura. What it is: a dark screen with FloraScore as a large round gauge and a small ring for each part of the score: Timing, How often, Form and Comfort. Why do it: Oura users already know the layout, with the score as one big gauge. Why not: it breaks the chart rules in four places, starting with part rings that carry no numbers, and it reads as a copy. The four: part rings with no numbers that all look full, No BM drawn as a short bar, a morning clock that runs off the card on a phone, and one Gold square for two different changes. The other real option is Oura on Today’s FloraScore and week, with everything else in Florin charts. We recommend Florin charts everywhere, Easy read only in the Easy version. The two cards here are its FloraScore gauge and the four parts of the score this week, drawn from the same logs as every other style. Try it: Decisions, Data style, Oura, then the Today and Insights tabs. Picking Oura turns the demo dark, unless you’ve picked a theme or Easy is on. The demo shows one style at a time, and detail pages don’t change. To see every style on every card, open the style gallery: https://claude.ai/artifact/FdsxDLSQqETo3HRCVmUZzL.

    Oura is the look built from the Oura screens Will sent on Oct 27 (reference/oura.md), under Florin’s rules. Dark first, on Forest, with an atmosphere made from the brand colours instead of a photo: a Forest to Teal to Plum glow with a horizon at the foot of the gauge. The reading is the ink, Cream on dark; your usual is Ray on dark and Plum on light; a shift is Gold and always comes with its word. State words are read against the person’s own usual, never the grades Oura uses. The FloraScore card: an open arc gauge of 252 degrees on a thin track. Her usual range, 74 to 82, is a Ray band on the track with both ends written beside it. The Cream arc sweeps to 83, and the Gold marker sits just past the band because today is a little above her usual range. Under the numeral, the state word in small caps: “A little above your usual”. Then the one big thing about today in Lora, worked out from today’s logs: “Your morning landed in your usual window, in a form within your usual” (her first BM came at 7:42 AM, inside her usual 7:15 to 8:45 AM, in a form within her usual forms). Last, the plain line that FloraScore compares you with you and isn’t a medical score. In the app the band and the arc sweep in, the numeral counts up, then the marker and the state word land. Left off the slide for room at 24 px text: the four shortcut rings above the gauge, for Timing, How often, Form and Comfort. They carry no numerals, since four parts in the 90s over an 83 would read as a contradiction; each ring’s colour says how that part sits against its own usual, and all four are within it today. Also left off, the line under the headline: “Your usual range is 74 to 82. Bloating logged at 3:10 PM, mild.” The Contributors card: this week’s four parts as rows, each with its state word and a track from 70 to 100, that part’s usual as a Ray band, this week as the Cream marker and last week as a small ring. Timing 95 (usual 81 to 97, 88 last week), How often 97 (90 to 97, 97 last week), Form 94 (85 to 98, 98 last week), Comfort 93 (83 to 97, 88 last week): all four within their usual. Timing moved her FloraScore most since last week, up 3.0 points, and this week’s FloraScore averages 83 against 80 last week. Each row opens what the part is and how much it weighs. In the app the bands and last week fade in, the markers land one by one and the numbers count up. Left off this card for room: the Teal “Since last week” above the headline, the line with this week’s average, the line under each track with that part’s usual and its change (up 3.0 points, same as last week, down 1.3, up 1.3), and the rest of the closing line, which says each part reads you against your own usual, changes are in FloraScore points and FloraScore isn’t a medical score. The card is shown on the dark theme; on the light theme it turns Cream with Plum for the usual, and the FloraScore card stays the one dark card. Strength: built straight from the reference, which is the Oura tie-in Will asked for when he kept FloraScore, and every picture has a sentence that says it in words. Cost, our read: most of a 0 to 100 arc goes unused, so her usual is a short stretch near the end and a one-point change barely moves the marker. Dark first also means two grounds to design and check. Also in the look: trends as smooth lines over the usual band with zone names on the right edge; a check-in card on the week when a day shifted (“On Friday, one BM was firmer than your usual forms. Anything different the day before?” with tag chips); and Timing as a curved clock of the morning.

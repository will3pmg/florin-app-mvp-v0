Florin app refresh · Decisions · Oct 27

12

The Version toggle: Standard or Easy. Easy is for older users. What changes in Easy, from the stage two spec: type about 1.35 times larger, body text 18 to 20 px and answers 28 to 32 px in the phone; controls at least 56 px; text in full-strength ink with no faint grey; more line spacing; larger toasts and sheets; a tab bar with labels (Today, History, Ask and plus), with Insights folded into Today as “This week”. Today becomes a greeting with the name, one big plain sentence about today, FloraScore as a big number with plain words and no arcs to decode, and three big buttons: “I had a BM”, “Something feels off” and “What I ate”. Log a BM becomes one question per screen: what it looked like, when (just now, earlier today, yesterday), anything else (sudden urge, and blood, mucus or black with the flag and the alarm routing intact), then Save, with a step counter. The design basis is older adults’ readability guidance: at least 16 px body text, 7 to 1 contrast where possible, 44 to 56 px targets, one task per screen, labels on icons. The mock on the left uses Sarah’s figures from the model: FloraScore 83, a little above her usual range of 74 to 82. The decision: a separate version, or a setting. Our recommendation, from the spec: a setting, “Larger text and simpler screens”, offered during onboarding and kept under Display in Profile. One app, so nothing has to be kept in step, and anyone can turn it on. In the spec, the Easy version uses Easy read whatever the data style; it is still a question for Will.

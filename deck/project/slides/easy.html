Decision 8 of 9

Should the app offer larger text and simpler screens?

This decides whether there’s an easier version of the whole app, and how a person gets it.

Standard

FloraScore

What it is: The app as the other decisions describe it: the FloraScore card, the week, charts and the full Insights tab.

Why do it: It shows the most, for people who want the detail.

Why not: Small type and many cards and charts are hard with reading glasses or a shaky hand.

Easy

We recommend

FloraScore

BM

What it is: Larger text and big labelled buttons: one sentence and FloraScore as a big number on Today, three buttons to log from, and a BM in four short steps.

Why do it: Older people, or anyone who wants less on screen, get the same logs and numbers, said more simply.

Why not: It’s a second set of screens to design, test and clear with counsel, and it shows less of the data.

We recommend Easy as a setting anyone can turn on or off, “Larger text and simpler screens”, not a separate version, with Standard as the default. One app underneath keeps the same logs, so a person can switch either way and lose nothing.

Still open: Is offering it during set-up enough, or should Florin also offer it when the phone’s text is set large?

    Florin charts, the first option on the Data style toggle, and the one we recommend. What it is: the dark green FloraScore card with the usual range as a bar, the week as a ring a day with its FloraScore under it, and plain bar and dot charts. Why do it: each card says the person’s usual in words before the chart, and every card works the same way. Why not: it’s the most text of the six, with small charts on every card. We recommend one look everywhere, Florin charts, with Easy read only in the Easy version (decision 8). A card and its detail page then draw the same day the same way, and Data style folds into Version. The two cards here are the top of Today in Florin charts: the dark green FloraScore card, with the usual range as a band on a bar and today’s dot on it, then Today’s week card: the Rings week you picked on November 4, now drawn in Florin charts too, with your three changes. No dates over it, since the rings carry them; a day with nothing logged is an empty ring with Add, which opens plus for that day; and each day’s FloraScore sits under its ring with no line naming it. The answer comes first, 4 of 6 days within the usual, with the days outside it named (Wednesday and Friday); then a ring a day (Plum within, Gold outside), today’s column shaded, and a Teal square for symptoms. Both are drawn from the same logs as every other style. Try it: Decisions, Data style, Florin charts, then the Today and Insights tabs.

The Home toggle: Score first, which is today’s Today, or Day first. Score first follows Oura’s order: the FloraScore hero with its usual range, the one-sentence highlight, the check-in card when it’s due, today’s entries, the week as counts, and one next step. Marcus, Alex and the strategist rated Today 4 to 5 as the daily glance. Day first leads with today’s timeline: the date with a compact FloraScore chip that opens its detail, today’s entries with times, the most notable one marked (a flag, a shift or a strong symptom), a row of log buttons, then the highlight, the week card and the check-in card. Why Day first exists: Sarah’s finding in the review, which the review reproduced. After logging a Watery BM, a Loose one and strong cramping with belly pain, the hero went from 83 to “77, Within your usual range”, still showed “+2 vs last week”, and the sentence still led with the morning’s BM. For someone who has been told it’s just stress, that reads as her own app waving off a rough evening. Both mocks show that rough evening on Sarah’s seeded day, Sunday October 11. Her day as seeded: a Formed BM at 7:42 AM, breakfast at 8:05 AM, lunch at 12:40 PM and mild bloating at 3:10 PM, FloraScore 83. Then, as in the review: a Watery BM at 6:30 PM, strong cramping and moderate belly pain at 7:00 PM, and a Loose BM at 7:15 PM. FloraScore becomes 77, still inside her usual 74 to 82, and the app’s own sentence still reads “Your first BM came at 7:42 AM, inside your usual 7:15 to 8:45 AM window, and two more since.” Score first shows exactly that. Day first leads with the evening’s rows, the 7:00 PM symptoms marked with a Gold edge, and the score as a chip. Source: the model at commit 475f259 (L.add for the three entries, L.score, L.scoreUsual, RU.highlight). Our recommendation is a lean, for Will to decide: keep Score first, and take the review’s fix, so the highlight leads with the most notable thing logged today and what it did to the score, computed from the logs. Day first stays in the demo to try with people like Sarah.

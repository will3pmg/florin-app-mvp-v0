8

The Home toggle: Score first, which is today’s Today, or Day first. Score first follows Oura’s order: the FloraScore hero with its usual range, the one-sentence highlight, the check-in card when it’s due, today’s entries, the week as counts, and one next step. The highlight now leads with what stood out today, ranked as Day first ranks it (a flag as the Teal note, then a shift from the usual, then symptoms by strength), and with nothing that stood out, how the day’s timing sits next to the usual. Marcus, Alex and the strategist rated Today 4 to 5 as the daily glance. Day first leads with today’s timeline: the date with a compact FloraScore chip that opens its detail, its range word under it, then today’s entries with times. The one that stands out is marked with an edge and a tag in its tone: a flag first (Teal, routed to primary care), then a shift from the usual (Gold), then the strongest symptom. Under the entries, Log a BM with the seven tiles (one tap saves it), Symptoms and What you ate, then “What stood out today”, the week card and the check-in card. Why Day first exists: Sarah’s finding in the review, which the review reproduced. After logging a Watery BM, a Loose one and strong cramping with belly pain, the hero went from 83 to “77, Within your usual range”, still showed “+2 vs last week”, and the sentence still led with the morning’s BM. For someone who has been told it’s just stress, that reads as her own app waving off a rough evening. The sentence is fixed now; the hero still reads 77, within her usual range, with +2 vs last week. Both mocks show that rough evening on Sarah’s seeded day, Sunday October 11. Her day as seeded: a Formed BM at 7:42 AM, breakfast at 8:05 AM, lunch at 12:40 PM and mild bloating at 3:10 PM, FloraScore 83. Then, as in the review: a Watery BM at 6:30 PM, strong cramping and moderate belly pain at 7:00 PM, and a Loose BM at 7:15 PM. FloraScore becomes 77, still inside her usual 74 to 82. Score first’s sentence used to read “Your first BM came at 7:42 AM, inside your usual 7:15 to 8:45 AM window, and two more since.” With the fix built it reads “Your 6:30 PM BM was Watery, outside your usual forms, Formed to Loose. Without it, FloraScore would be 80.”, which is what the mock shows: the second sentence comes with membership, and on Free the sentence stops after the first. Day first shows the evening’s rows and marks the 6:30 PM Watery BM with a Gold edge and “Outside your usual forms”. A shift outranks a symptom, so the 7:00 PM cramping and belly pain, moderate to strong, is not marked. Its “What stood out today” reads: “Your 6:30 PM BM was Watery, outside your usual forms, Formed to Loose. FloraScore is 77, 6 lower than yesterday’s 83. Without the 6:30 PM BM, it would be 80.” Whether a strong symptom should outrank a shift is one of the brief’s open Home questions. Source: the model at commit 475f259 (L.add for the three entries, L.score, L.scoreUsual), RU.highlight and Day first as built on Oct 28. Our recommendation is a lean, for Will to decide: keep Score first, with the review’s fix, so the highlight leads with the most notable thing logged today, computed from the logs. That lead is built, and with membership the sentence goes on to say what that log did to FloraScore, as Day first’s does; on Free it stops at the lead. Day first stays in the demo to try with people like Sarah.

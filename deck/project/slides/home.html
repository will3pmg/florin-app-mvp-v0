What does Today show first?

This decides the top of the Today tab, the screen the app opens on.

Score first

We recommend

What it is: Today opens on the FloraScore card, with the person’s usual range and one sentence on what stood out today, then today’s logs and the last 7 days.

Why do it: One number for the day, read against the person’s own usual, is the quickest thing to check.

Why not: There’s no score before day 14, and on a rough evening a score inside the usual range can read as if nothing happened.

Day first

What it is: Today opens on the date and today’s logs in time order, with the one that stood out marked, and FloraScore as a small chip at the top.

Why do it: It suits people who open the app to log, and it looks the same from day 1, before Florin has a score.

Why not: FloraScore, the number at the centre of Florin, is easy to miss.

Compact

What it is: Today opens on a short FloraScore strip, then this week as a row of days with arrows for earlier weeks, then the logs of the day picked, today’s first.

Why do it: It’s built for getting around, as the current app’s home is: the week is in view, and any day, with its logs and a way to add to it, is one tap away.

Why not: No sentence says what stood out today or how the week went, so a day outside the usual shows only as a Gold ring until the person taps it.

We recommend Score first. FloraScore is the centre of Florin, so it should come first, and its sentence says what stood out today, which Compact leaves out.

Still open: Should Compact replace Score first as the default? It doesn’t change our recommendation yet; the focus test puts the two side by side.

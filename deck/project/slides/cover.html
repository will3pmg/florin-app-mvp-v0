The decisions

Every decision is a toggle in the demo.

For Will · Florin Health

This deck is the decisions from the Florin app refresh, laid out one per slide. The point to land first: these are not three demos any more. Each choice is a toggle in the one demo, so Will can flip it and see the whole app change, then decide. What to say: we took the three earlier demos apart. Consistency A, B and C, BM logging, Home, History, the weekly check-in, the meal sheet, the data style, the Easy version and onboarding each sit in the demo’s left bar as a toggle. The deck walks the same decisions in order: logging, screens, data styles, then the business calls. The last slide is the list of what we need from Will, with our recommendation for each. Sources: the brief (claude/Florin app refresh brief.md), the feature review (five reviews: three synthetic personas, a design critic and a membership strategist), the stage two specs, and the model in the prototype. Every figure in the deck is computed from the model (refresh-data.js and looks-data.js at commit 475f259) or quoted from the review. The demo’s seeded day is Sunday, October 11, 2026.

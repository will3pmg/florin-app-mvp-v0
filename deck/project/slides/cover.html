For Will · Florin Health

This deck is one sheet of decisions for the Florin app refresh, laid out a slide each. The list on the right is the nine decisions, in the order they sit in the demo’s left bar. First, how the demo works, then the nine decisions on one page. Then each decision gets its own slide with the same shape: the question, what it decides, each option with what it is, why do it and why not, what we recommend and why, at most one question still open, and where to try it in the demo. The data style decision has a slide for each of its six looks. After the nine: how Ask Florin answers, and its one open call for the free plan. Then four more to settle: membership, FloraScore, integrations and messaging. The last slide is what we need from you. Every recommendation is yours to confirm.

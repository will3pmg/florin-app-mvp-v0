How much do we ask a new person?

This decides the set-up steps a new person goes through before they first see Today.

Short

We recommend

Next

What it is: 8 steps in under two minutes: welcome, first name, what brings you, what you’d like Florin to do, periods, reminders, what Florin is and isn’t, and ready.

Why do it: It asks only what changes the app on day 1, so people start logging sooner.

Why not: Florin knows less at the start, and the rest (eating, foods to watch, medicines) has to be asked later.

Full survey

Next

What it is: The current app’s survey in 14 steps, about three minutes, reworded in Florin’s voice, with a note to see a primary care doctor if a person picks blood or a black stool.

Why do it: Florin knows the most from day 1, and it stays close to the current app.

Why not: Most answers change nothing the person sees on day 1, and 14 steps is long.

We recommend Short, and asking the rest when it matters: eating habits at the first meal log, foods to watch when a food check starts, medicines at the first clinician report. Each answer then changes something the person can see.

Still open: Is the BM reminder worth a step on day 1?

Profile: Opened from the avatar on Today, it shows every set-up answer, and a person can change any of them.

How to use the demo while we talk. The demo is one page with three parts. Left: the decision toggles sit at the top of the left bar. Consistency A, B or C; BM logging Tiles or Full form; Home Score first or Day first; History List or Calendar; the weekly check-in On or Off; the meal sheet, Quick or One meal; the data style (As built, Rings, Field journal, Bento, Oura, Easy read); the Standard or Easy version; and onboarding Short or Full survey. Each has an info button that opens its note. Below the toggles, the Demo panel, folded to one line, holds the person, their logs, their meal logs, the plan (Member or Free), the theme and Reset; below that, the list of screens to jump to. Middle: the phone is live. Tap through it like the app. Changing a toggle redraws the phone in place, so you can see the same person’s data under each option. Right: the notes explain the decision you just changed, with what each option costs and what we recommend. Each screen also keeps its own notes. The demo link: https://claude.ai/artifact/8Y7riZMHdzy5BqV4HXzeFB. The data looks library, where each data style is drawn from the same numbers, is https://claude.ai/artifact/FdsxDLSQqETo3HRCVmUZzL.

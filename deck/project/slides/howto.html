2

The demo is one page: the phone in the middle, the left bar, and notes on the right. The left bar has three parts. 1. Decisions change the app. These are the nine choices in this deck: Consistency, BM logging, Home, History, Weekly check-in, Meal sheet, Data style, Version and Onboarding. Change one and the phone redraws, so you can see the same person under each option. Each has an info button that opens its note. 2. Demo changes the person, not the app. It sits under Decisions, folded to one line; open it to see its controls. Person, Logs, Plan and Theme change who you see and how, not the design. Person switches between three demo people, each with their own logs and their own usual. Logs shows a person who has used Florin for a while, a person on day 5 while Florin is still learning their usual, or day 1, right after set-up, with nothing logged. Plan is Member or Free. Theme is Light or Dark. Reset puts the demo person back as they were. Demo person’s meals sets how often this person wrote meals down: Every day, Only with symptoms or Never. It is not a feature of the app. Only with symptoms shows a trap. If someone writes meals down only on days with symptoms, every meal they log sits next to a day with symptoms. With no ordinary days to compare, everyday foods can look linked to symptoms. That’s why Insights holds food patterns back until meals are logged on at least 6 in 10 of the days a person logs anything, and offers a 14-day food check instead: log what you ate every day for two weeks, ordinary days included. FloraScore never uses meals, so it doesn’t change. To see it: Demo, Demo person’s meals, Only with symptoms, then the Insights tab. 3. Screens jumps to any screen. Pick one and the phone opens on it. The notes on the right explain the screen you’re on, or the decision you just changed. Open the demo: https://claude.ai/artifact/8Y7riZMHdzy5BqV4HXzeFB

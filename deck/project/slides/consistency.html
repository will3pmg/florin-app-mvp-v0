Florin app refresh · Decisions · Oct 27

4

Consistency is one question with three ways to answer it, and in the demo it is one toggle: A, B or C. The phone redraws Log a BM, Today and Insights for each. What to say about each: A is the numbered 1 to 7 scale clinicians use. It is a Rome Foundation instrument, so shipping it means a licence and its own wording, word for word. B is today’s five words without the slider: Hard, Firm, Formed, Loose, Watery. No licence question, but it records five steps, not seven. Hard covers steps 1 and 2, Loose covers 5 and 6. C is seven words of our own: Pellets, Hard, Firm, Formed, Soft, Loose, Watery. No numbers and no scale name in the app. It needs counsel to read the words before it ships, because they describe the same seven forms. What B does to the data, using Alex, last 30 days (38 BMs). In C his most common form is Formed (16 BMs) and his usual is Firm to Soft. In B his most common form is Loose (17 BMs), because B folds Soft and Loose into one word, and his usual becomes Formed or Loose. The BMs outside his usual flip direction: C reads 6 as looser than his usual, B reads 4 as firmer and 1 as looser. His FloraScore today moves from 84 in C (and A) to 83 in B. Sarah’s Form detail reads Loose in B but Formed in C over the same 30 days. Recommendation: C. A and C store the same step from 1 to 7, so once licensed, a clinician export can add the numbers without changing anyone’s data. One request to the Rome Foundation can cover that export and the measure for the NSF study arm. Sources: Alex’s figures from the model at commit 475f259 (FLD.build for Alex in B and in C, 12 weeks, every meal; L.score for today; 30 days is Sep 12 to Oct 11 in the demo). The recommendation and the counsel note are from the brief (Oct 14 calls) and the feature review (every lens that chose an option chose C).

Part 1, the app
1. Home screen: Compact
   Why: The week of day buttons is the fastest way around, and it matches the current app's home, so nobody has to learn a new map. The strip keeps FloraScore in view without taking the whole first screen.
2. History: List
   Why: The list is simpler to build and test, and every day reads the same way as on Today.
3. Logging a BM: Quick tiles and form
   Why: Quick tiles save a tap for the people who log most often, and the form is still one tap away.
4. Consistency words: Seven words
   Why: Seven of our own words avoid the licence question and still map to steps for a report later.
5. Weekly check-in: With the check-in
   Why: Four questions a week is light enough, and it is the one reason to come back each week.
6. Logging meals: Quick, voice or text
   Why: Voice is the only way most people will log a meal every day, and the food check needs every day.
7. Easy version: Add Easy
   Why: One setting is cheaper to keep than a second app, and the logs stay the same underneath.
8. Onboarding: Short
   Why: Asking later, when the answer changes something, keeps day 1 short and every question earns its place.

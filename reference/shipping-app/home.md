## What Compact keeps, and what Florin's rules change

Compact (Home's third option) follows this order: a short score strip, a tight week, then the day's logs. Where the
screen meets Florin's rules:

- **The score strip.** Compact keeps the word FloraScore over the number and Insights beside it. The bar becomes the
  person's usual range, labelled with its two numbers, with today's dot on it (the hero's bar, data viz.md section 4),
  so it says what it measures. "↓ 3 this week" becomes "Week average 3 below last week", with no arrow and no word like
  "down", and says it is the average, so it never reads as today's move beside today's number: plain text, never Gold
  (a change, not a reading). The number itself opens FloraScore's page. The streak goes: Florin doesn't reward
  logging, and a broken streak reads as a failure.
- **The week.** Compact's week runs Monday to Sunday, as Florin's days do, with the month and year between two arrows
  that stop at the first week and this week. A ring on a day says how it sat against the person's own usual (Plum
  within, Gold outside, Ink 3 not counted), not only that something was logged; a day with nothing logged is its date
  alone (Nov 12; it was an empty ring with "+ Add"), and a Teal square marks symptoms, with a legend of the marks on show. Days still to come show as
  faded dates, as here.
- **Today's logs.** Each BM is named in the Consistency option's words, with no coloured dot: green and orange are not
  app colours, and a form's colour would read as good or bad. "Normal" goes (Florin never says normal); a detail is
  read against the person's own usual, or not at all. The time sits in its own column, so it never wraps.
- **Navigation.** A tap on any day in the week shows that day's logs under it, with a way to add to it: Will's "more
  navigation".

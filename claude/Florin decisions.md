# Florin app: the decisions

One sheet for the nine decisions in the demo, then Ask Florin and the business calls. For each: the question, what each
option is, why you would pick it and why you wouldn't, and what we recommend. Every recommendation is yours to confirm.

The demo: https://claude.ai/artifact/8Y7riZMHdzy5BqV4HXzeFB. The **Decisions** panel on the left changes the app. The
**Demo** panel under it changes the demo person, their logs, their plan and light or dark, never the app's design (see
"The demo controls" at the end).

Two words used throughout:
- **Usual:** each person's own pattern, learned from their own logs. Florin starts reading against it on day 14, and
  never compares a person with anyone else.
- **FloraScore:** the day's score, made from four things a person logs: when the first BM comes, how often, consistency,
  and comfort (the symptoms they log). It never uses meals.

> **Not a decision: Demo person's meals.** This control, under Demo, changes the demo person's past, not the app:
> whether they wrote meals down Every day, Only with symptoms, or Never.
> It shows a trap. Meals written down only on days with symptoms leave no ordinary days to compare with, so everyday
> foods can look linked to symptoms. That's why Insights holds food patterns back until meals are logged on most days,
> and offers a 14-day food check instead: every meal logged for two weeks, ordinary days included.
> Try it: Demo, Demo person's meals, Only with symptoms, then the Insights tab. More in "What Demo person's meals
> means" at the end.

## 1. Consistency

**The question:** How does a person describe a BM's consistency?

**What it decides:** The words on the BM tiles, and how Today, History and Insights name each BM.

**A: numbers 1 to 7**
- What it is: Seven tiles, each a number from 1 to 7 with a short word, from the scale clinicians use.
- Why do it: Clinicians and other gut apps use these numbers, so a log reads straight across at a visit.
- Why not: The Rome Foundation licenses the scale, so we need a licence and must use its exact wording.

**B: five words**
- What it is: The five words the current app uses, as tiles: Hard, Firm, Formed, Loose and Watery.
- Why do it: People who use the current app already know them, and they need no licence.
- Why not: Hard and Loose each cover two kinds of stool, so Florin can't always tell when a BM has shifted from the
  person's usual.

**C: seven words**
- What it is: Seven tiles in our own words, with no numbers: Pellets, Hard, Firm, Formed, Soft, Loose and Watery.
- Why do it: All seven kinds in plain words, each with a number from 1 to 7 behind it that a clinician report can show
  once we have a licence.
- Why not: Counsel must clear the words first, since they follow the licensed scale's seven kinds, and people who use
  the current app learn two new words.

**We recommend C, once counsel has read its words, and dropping B.** It keeps the detail a clinician needs and the plain
words a first-timer can pick, without waiting for a licence.

**Open question:** Do we ask the Rome Foundation now for one licence covering the 1 to 7 numbers in clinician reports
and a questionnaire for the pilot study?

**Try it:** Decisions, Consistency. Switch between A, B and C, then tap plus to see the tiles. Today, History and
Insights change with it. In A, the words under the numbers are stand-ins until we have the licensed wording.

## 2. BM logging

**The question:** Should one tap on a tile save a BM?

**What it decides:** What happens when a person taps plus to log a BM.

**Quick tiles and form**
- What it is: Tap plus, then a tile, and the BM is saved, with a message that offers Add details and Undo.
- Why do it: A usual BM takes two taps, so logging every day stays easy.
- Why not: A BM logged an hour late gets the wrong time, and details like urgency get skipped.

**Full form only**
- What it is: The plus menu has one Log a BM row, and every BM goes through the form: when, consistency, details and a
  note.
- Why do it: Every BM shows its time before saving, with room for details.
- Why not: It takes more taps every time, and people who find logging slow log less.

**We recommend Quick tiles and form, and adding time buttons to the "BM saved" message: Just now, 30 min ago, 1 hr ago,
Earlier.** It keeps the two-tap log and fixes the wrong time without opening the form.

**Open question:** Should every BM open the form for the first two weeks, while Florin learns the person's usual? In the
demo, only the first 5 do.

**Try it:** Decisions, BM logging, then tap plus. The time buttons aren't in the demo yet.

## 3. Home

**The question:** What does Today show first?

**What it decides:** The top of the Today tab, the screen the app opens on.

**Score first**
- What it is: Today opens on the FloraScore card, with the person's usual range and one sentence on what stood out
  today, then today's logs and the last 7 days.
- Why do it: One number for the day, read against the person's own usual, is the quickest thing to check.
- Why not: There's no score before day 14, and on a rough evening a score inside the usual range can read as if nothing
  happened.

**Day first**
- What it is: Today opens on the date and today's logs in time order, with the one that stood out marked, and
  FloraScore as a small chip at the top.
- Why do it: It suits people who open the app to log, and it looks the same from day 1, before Florin has a score.
- Why not: FloraScore, the number at the centre of Florin, is easy to miss.

**We recommend Score first.** FloraScore is the centre of Florin, so it should come first, and its sentence already
says what stood out today.

**Open question:** When a strong symptom and a BM outside the person's usual land on the same day, which should the top
sentence mention first?

**Try it:** Decisions, Home, then the Today tab. The demo opens on Score first.

## 4. History

**The question:** How should History show past days?

**What it decides:** The History tab, where people look back and fill in a day they missed.

**List**
- What it is: Every day, newest first, with its logs, and a day with nothing logged shown as a gap with Add to this day.
- Why do it: Every log is right there to read, and filling in a missed day takes one tap.
- Why not: After a few months it's a long scroll, and gaps and days outside the usual are hard to spot.

**Calendar**
- What it is: A month on one screen: a Plum circle for a day within the person's usual, a Gold circle for a day
  outside it, and the date alone for a day with nothing logged.
- Why do it: A whole month fits on one screen, so gaps and days outside the usual show at a glance.
- Why not: Seeing a day's logs takes a tap, and a month of circles says less than the list about what each day held.

**We recommend Calendar.** One screen a month replaces a long scroll, and a tapped day reads just as it does in the
list.

**Open question:** Keep a switch on History so a person can pick the list or the calendar?

**Try it:** Decisions, History, then the History tab, and tap a day. The demo opens on List.

## 5. Weekly check-in

**The question:** Should the app ask how the week felt?

**What it decides:** Whether the app has a weekly check-in: a few questions about the past week.

**Our answer:** keep it. Four one-tap questions a week, in our own words, not a licensed questionnaire. Free for
everyone.

**What the check-in is**
- Four questions about the last 7 days, each answered with one tap, in about a minute:
  - How much belly pain did you have this week? (None, A little, Some, A lot)
  - On how many days did your gut get in the way of your plans? (None, 1 or 2 days, 3 or 4 days, 5 or more days)
  - How much bloating did you have this week? (None, A little, Some, A lot)
  - How settled did your bowel habits feel this week? (Settled, Mostly settled, Up and down, Unsettled)
- Then an optional line: tags such as Travel, Stress or Short nights, and a note.
- Florin compares each answer with the person's first three weeks and says About the same, More than at first, or Less
  than at first. No score.
- It's due once a week, on a day the person picks (Sunday evening to start). It shows as a card on Today and an item in
  the plus menu, with one reminder. A person can skip a week, and it never blocks logging or Insights.

**On**
- What it is: The check-in card on Today when it's due, an item in the plus menu every week, and its trend on Insights.
- Why do it: It's the one place Florin asks how the week felt, not just what happened, and it gives people a reason to
  come back each week.
- Why not: It's one more task a week, and in a steady week the answers add little that the logs didn't show.

**Off**
- What it is: No check-in anywhere: no Today card, no plus item, no Insights card and no reminder.
- Why do it: The app asks less of people, and the week's logs already show what happened.
- Why not: Nothing asks how the week felt, so a hard week shows only as logs.

**We recommend On, free on both plans.** It takes a minute and is the app's one weekly reason to come back.

**Open question:** Should a person be able to turn it off themselves, in Profile?

**Try it:** Decisions, Weekly check-in. With it on, tap Start on the Today card. With it off, it leaves every screen.

## 6. Meal sheet

**The question:** How does a person log what they ate?

**What it decides:** The meal row in the plus menu (What you ate, or Meal when One meal at a time is picked) and the
sheet it opens.

Not the same as "Demo person's meals" under Demo, which only changes how often the demo person wrote meals down. The
box at the top explains the trap it shows.

**Quick, voice or text**
- What it is: One big microphone button and one box for the whole day: say or type what you ate, and Florin picks out
  the foods.
- Why do it: It's the least effort, so logging meals on ordinary days, not only on days with symptoms, stays easy.
- Why not: The day has no meal times, so Florin can't look at what tends to follow a late dinner.

**One meal at a time**
- What it is: Pick Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Other, type what you had, and set the time.
- Why do it: Each meal keeps its time, so Florin can look at what tends to follow a late dinner.
- Why not: It's more steps for every meal, so people log fewer of them.

**We recommend Quick, with a one-tap "Dinner after 8 PM?" button when the person mentions dinner, and a free 14-day
food check (log every meal for 14 days, ordinary days included).** People tend to write meals down only on days with
symptoms, which can make everyday foods look linked to them, so logging has to be easy enough for every day.

**Try it:** Decisions, Meal sheet, then tap plus and the meal row. The microphone plays a sample, since the demo can't
use one. The dinner button isn't in the demo yet.

## 7. Data style

**The question:** How should Florin draw the numbers?

**What it decides:** How FloraScore, the week and the Insights cards look, with the same numbers from the same logs in
every style.

Two of the six are the real contenders: Oura and Field journal, which we recommend using together. The other four are
there to compare.

**Florin charts**
- What it is: The dark green FloraScore card with the usual range as a bar, the last 7 days as one circle per day, and
  plain bar and dot charts.
- Why do it: Each card says the person's usual in words and gives a count before the chart, and every card works the
  same way.
- Why not: It's the most text of the six, with small charts on every card.

**Rings**
- What it is: FloraScore as a ring that fills up, with the person's usual range marked on it, and each day of the week
  as its own small ring.
- Why do it: Rings read at a glance, and a ring for each day makes a week easy to count.
- Why not: Rings are hard to compare across cards, and a ring a day gets small over 30 days.

**Field journal**
- What it is: The numbers written out, with plain line and bar charts with labelled axes, each with a one-line caption.
- Why do it: It's the most exact, so it suits detail pages and a clinician report.
- Why not: It's the most reading, and its small captions are hard for older eyes.

**Bento**
- What it is: Tiles of different sizes, one number each.
- Why do it: Easy to take in at a glance, like phone widgets.
- Why not: Tiles show numbers but not how they connect, and they crowd when text is large.

**Oura**
- What it is: A dark screen with FloraScore as a large round gauge, one line about today, and a small ring for each
  part of the score: Timing, How often, Form and Comfort.
- Why do it: It shows the score and what it's made from most clearly, in a layout Oura users already know.
- Why not: It looks like Oura's app, so it needs Florin's own colours and shapes, or it reads as a copy.

**Easy read**
- What it is: One big number and one plain sentence on each card, in large type.
- Why do it: The easiest to read, for anyone with reading glasses or little patience for charts.
- Why not: It leaves out the shape of the data, so it's thin for someone who wants depth.

**We recommend the Oura style on Today and FloraScore, Field journal on detail pages, and Easy read only in the Easy
version (decision 8).** Oura shows the score and its parts most clearly, and Field journal is the most exact where
people go for detail.

**Open question:** Mix styles by screen like this, or one style everywhere?

**Try it:** Decisions, Data style, then the Today and Insights tabs. Florin charts is the first option on the toggle.
Picking Oura turns the demo dark, unless you've picked a theme or Easy is on. The demo shows one style at a time, and
detail pages don't change. To see every style on every card, open the style gallery:
https://claude.ai/artifact/FdsxDLSQqETo3HRCVmUZzL.

## 8. Version

**The question:** Should the app offer larger text and simpler screens?

**What it decides:** Whether there's an easier version of the whole app, and how a person gets it.

**Standard**
- What it is: The app as described in the rest of this sheet: the FloraScore card, the week, charts and the full
  Insights tab.
- Why do it: It shows the most, for people who want the detail.
- Why not: Small type and many cards and charts are hard with reading glasses or a shaky hand.

**Easy**
- What it is: Larger text and big labelled buttons: one sentence and FloraScore as a big number on Today, three buttons
  to log from, and a BM in four short steps.
- Why do it: Older people, or anyone who wants less on screen, get the same logs and numbers, said more simply.
- Why not: It's a second set of screens to design, test and clear with counsel, and it shows less of the data.

**We recommend Easy as a setting anyone can turn on or off, "Larger text and simpler screens", not a separate version,
with Standard as the default.** One app underneath keeps the same logs, so a person can switch either way and lose
nothing.

**Open question:** Is offering it during set-up enough, or should Florin also offer it when the phone's text is set
large?

**Try it:** Decisions, Version, Easy. With Easy on, Home and Data style don't apply, because Easy has its own Today and
charts. Set-up offers it on its first and last steps, and it's in Profile, from the avatar on Today.

## 9. Onboarding

**The question:** How much do we ask a new person?

**What it decides:** The set-up steps a new person goes through before they first see Today.

**Short**
- What it is: 8 steps in under two minutes: welcome, first name, what brings you, what you'd like Florin to do,
  periods, reminders, what Florin is and isn't, and ready.
- Why do it: It asks only what changes the app on day 1, so people start logging sooner.
- Why not: Florin knows less at the start, and the rest (eating, foods to watch, medicines) has to be asked later.

**Full survey**
- What it is: The current app's survey in 14 steps, about three minutes, reworded in Florin's voice, with a note to
  talk to their primary care doctor if someone picks blood or a black stool.
- Why do it: Florin knows the most from day 1, and it stays close to the current app.
- Why not: Most answers change nothing the person sees on day 1, and 14 steps is long.

**We recommend Short, and asking the rest when it matters: eating habits at the first meal log, foods to watch when a
food check starts, medicines at the first clinician report.** Each answer then changes something the person can see.

**Open question:** Is the BM reminder worth a step on day 1?

**Try it:** Decisions, Onboarding. Or open either version from Screens, under Onboarding and profile.

## Ask Florin on Free

**The question:** Do people on Free get any Ask Florin answers?

**What it decides:** What the Ask tab does on the free plan.

**Members only**
- What it is: On Free, the Ask tab explains what Ask Florin does and the questions people ask, and offers membership.
- Why do it: It's the simplest: no free answers to pay for or check.
- Why not: People never see an answer from their own numbers before they're asked to pay.

**One free answer a week**
- What it is: On Free, a person gets one answered question a week from their own numbers, and questions about whether
  to see a doctor are answered on any plan.
- Why do it: People see what Ask Florin does with their logs before paying, which makes the case for membership.
- Why not: Each free answer costs us money to run, and some people will find one a week enough and never pay.

**We recommend one free answer a week, with questions about seeing a doctor always answered.** One answer from a
person's own logs sells membership better than a locked tab.

**Try it:** Demo, Plan, Free, then the Ask tab. The demo keeps Ask Florin locked on Free. Switch Plan to Member to ask
a question.

## Also to decide

- **Membership.** Free keeps logging, Today, FloraScore, the weekly check-in, History and 7 days of Insights.
  Membership adds the why: why the score moved, 30 and 90 days, patterns, the food check's results, Ask Florin and the
  full clinician report. We suggest Free also gets a basic report (the last 30 days of logs, without the patterns or
  90 days that membership adds), the start of a food check and log export. We suggest membership costs under $10 a
  month. The exact price is yours.
- **FloraScore.** We suggest approving its four parts and their weights: when the first BM comes 30, how often 25,
  consistency 25, comfort 20. We also suggest calling it "FloraScore, from your logs", and showing each part as points
  that add up to the score.
- **Integrations.** Apple Health, Oura and cycle apps, now or later? We suggest connecting them is free, and the
  patterns Florin finds from them are in membership. None of it works in the demo. Profile lists the three apps as Not
  connected.
- **Messaging.** How do we describe this app, which asks people to log by hand, before Florin One (the sensor)
  launches? No recommendation yet. The deck has three directions to react to.

## The demo controls

The Demo panel sits under Decisions, folded to one line. Open it to change the demo person, their logs, their plan and
light or dark. None of these controls changes the app's design.

- **Person:** switches between three demo people. Each has their own logs and their own usual, so you can see the app on
  different patterns (in the words of Consistency C):
  - Sarah: first BM usually 7:15 AM to 8:45 AM, usual forms Formed to Loose, symptoms on about 1 day in 3. Possible
    pattern: Pizza, then a looser first BM.
  - Marcus: first BM usually 7:15 AM to 8:30 AM, usual forms Formed to Loose, symptoms on about 1 day in 3. Possible
    pattern: Curry, then bloating the next day.
  - Alex: first BM usually 7:15 AM to 8:45 AM, usual forms Firm to Soft, symptoms on about 1 day in 2. Possible
    pattern: Beans, then bloating the next day.
- **Logs:** how long the demo person has used Florin.
  - 12 weeks: a person who has used Florin for a while.
  - New, day 5: day 5. Florin is still learning their usual, so there's no FloraScore until day 14.
  - Just joined: day 1, right after set-up, with nothing logged.
- **Demo person's meals:** how often the demo person wrote meals down: Every day, Only with symptoms, or Never. It's
  there to show a trap, explained below.
- **Plan:** Member or Free, to show what each plan gets.
- **Theme:** Light or Dark.
- **Reset the demo:** puts the demo person back as they were, and clears anything you logged.

### What Demo person's meals means

Demo person's meals is not a feature of the app. It changes the demo person's past: whether they wrote meals down every
day, only on days they also logged a symptom, or never.

It shows a trap. If someone writes meals down only on days with symptoms, every meal they log is on a day with
symptoms. There are no ordinary days to compare with, so everyday foods can look linked to symptoms. In the demo, for
the person the demo opens on, logs like that would pass Florin's test for "Toast, then bloating the next day", and the
same for avocado, only because of which days got written down. Pizza, the food their symptoms do tend to follow, would
drop out.

So Insights holds food patterns back until meals are logged on at least 6 in 10 of the days the person logs anything.
Below that, it offers a 14-day food check instead: log what you ate every day for two weeks, ordinary days included, so
Florin has ordinary days to compare. FloraScore never uses meals, so it doesn't change.

**Try it:** Demo, Demo person's meals, Only with symptoms, then the Insights tab. The patterns card holds food patterns
back and offers the food check. In the demo, Start only describes the check, and on Free it opens membership.

What this means for the app: the meal sheet (decision 6) and the food check are our answer to this habit.
